STAR Methods（详细方法）

以下为论文 STAR Methods 的完整小节，按原文顺序汇总。

Human specimens and study cohorts（人体样本与研究队列）

This study integrated seven study cohorts to develop, validate, and clinically evaluate the CANVAS framework. A total of 457 lung tumor specimens were profiled by CODEX using a 41-plex antibody panel across three cohorts: tissue microarray cohort 1 (TMA1, n = 337; C1), tissue microarray cohort 2 (TMA2, n = 110; C2), and a whole-slide imaging cohort (WSI, n = 10; C3) (Table S2). TMA1 samples were profiled by CODEX alone, whereas TMA2 and WSI tumors underwent paired CODEX profiling and matched H&E staining. Collectively, these cohorts were used to define cellular neighborhoods from CODEX data. The paired WSI cohort was used to train and validate the H&E-based habitat inference model, which was further evaluated in the independent paired TMA2 cohort. For downstream clinical evaluation using H&E alone, we analyzed three lung cancer cohorts, TCGA (n = 945), PLCO (n = 382), and NLST (n = 366), comprising 1,693 patients (C4) in total (Figure 4C), to assess associations between predicted tumor habitats, patient prognosis, and molecular subtypes. We further extended the analysis to a pan-cancer TCGA cohort spanning eight cancer types (n = 3,724; C5) to evaluate cross-cancer generalizability. Finally, two independent immunotherapy-treated cohorts were used for outcome modeling: an internal Stanford ICB cohort (n = 149; C6) and a lung cancer cohort from the Cancer Moonshot Biobank (n = 40, including 34 PFS-evaluable patients; C7). Stanford specimens were analyzed in accordance with applicable datause requirements, whereas TMA2 was obtained from TissueArray. Publicly available or controlled-access cohorts, including TCGA, PLCO, NLST, and CMB, were accessed and analyzed in accordance with their respective data-use policies. In summary, CANVAS comprises three integral components: (1) discovery of biologically grounded CNs from CODEX data; (2) inference of CODEX-derived habitats from H&E slides; and (3) derivation of spatial habitat features for prognostic modeling and biological interpretation. A schematic overview of the study design, patient cohorts, and analytical workflow is provided in Figure 1A.

本研究整合了 7 个研究队列，用于开发、验证并进行 CANVAS 框架的临床评估。三个队列共包含 457 份肺肿瘤标本，均使用 41 重抗体面板进行 CODEX 分析：组织芯片队列 1（TMA1，n = 337；C1）、组织芯片队列 2（TMA2，n = 110；C2）和全切片成像队列（WSI，n = 10；C3）（Table S2）。TMA1 仅进行 CODEX 分析，TMA2 和 WSI 则同时进行配对 CODEX 与匹配 H&E 染色。这些队列共同用于从 CODEX 数据定义细胞邻域。配对 WSI 队列用于训练和验证基于 H&E 的生境推断模型，配对 TMA2 队列作为独立评估数据。下游仅使用 H&E 的临床评估包括三个肺癌队列：TCGA（n = 945）、PLCO（n = 382）和 NLST（n = 366），合计 1,693 例患者（C4）（Figure 4C），用于评估预测肿瘤生境与患者预后和分子亚型的关联。研究还扩展至包含 8 种癌症、3,724 例患者的 TCGA 泛癌队列（C5），评估跨癌种泛化能力。最后，使用两个独立免疫治疗队列进行结局建模：内部 Stanford ICB 队列（n = 149；C6）和 Cancer Moonshot Biobank 肺癌队列（n = 40，其中 34 例可评估 PFS；C7）。Stanford 标本按照适用的数据使用要求进行分析，TMA2 购自 TissueArray；TCGA、PLCO、NLST 和 CMB 等公开或受控访问队列均按照各自数据使用政策获取和分析。总体上，CANVAS 包含三个核心组成：（1）从 CODEX 数据发现具有生物学依据的 CN；（2）从 H&E 切片推断 CODEX 衍生生境；（3）提取空间生境特征，用于预后建模和生物学解释。研究设计、患者队列和分析流程见 Figure 1A。

Sample preparation and tissue sectioning（样本制备与组织切片）

Formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded (FFPE) tumor specimens from patients with NSCLC were used for CODEX imaging. For the Stanford TMA1 cohort, tumor regions were identified by pathology review. Representative tumor regions were sampled from archival donor blocks using tissue punches and arrayed into recipient TMA blocks. In addition, surgical FFPE tumor blocks from patients with NSCLC were sectioned as whole-slide specimens to construct the Stanford WSI cohort. For CODEX staining, 5-μm FFPE sections from TMAs and whole-slide specimens were mounted onto Fisherbrand Superfrost Plus microscope slides (Fisher Scientific) according to the PhenoCycler-Fusion User Guide v2.1.0 and processed for downstream staining.

用于 CODEX 成像的是 NSCLC 患者的福尔马林固定石蜡包埋（FFPE）肿瘤标本。对于 Stanford TMA1 队列，由病理医师审查并确定肿瘤区域，从存档供体蜡块中选择代表性肿瘤区域打孔，再排列至受体 TMA 蜡块。研究还对 NSCLC 手术 FFPE 肿瘤蜡块进行全切片切片，以构建 Stanford WSI 队列。CODEX 染色使用 5 μm 厚的 TMA 和全切片 FFPE 切片，按照 PhenoCycler-Fusion User Guide v2.1.0 安装在 Fisherbrand Superfrost Plus 载玻片上，并进入后续染色流程。

CODEX staining, imaging, and cell segmentation（CODEX 染色、成像与细胞分割）

Spatial proteomic profiling was performed at the Cell Sciences Imaging Facility (CSIF) at Stanford University using the PhenoCyclerFusion (PCF) 2.0 platform, formerly known as CODEX. The antibody panel included pre-conjugated CODEX antibodies obtained from Akoya Biosciences and custom-conjugated antibodies generated using the PhenoCycler Antibody Conjugation Kit (Akoya Biosciences) according to the manufacturer’s protocol. Antibody staining specificity and conjugation performance were routinely validated by immunofluorescence and PhenoCycler-Fusion test runs. Staining conditions were optimized individually for each antibody. Antibodies used in this study are listed in Table S1. FFPE sections from TMAs and whole-slide specimens were stained using the PhenoCycler Staining Kit. Briefly, after overnight baking at 60◦C, slides were deparaffinized using a standard histological procedure. Antigen retrieval was conducted in Tris-EDTA buffer (pH 9.0) in a pressure cooker at 120◦C for 20 min under high pressure. After cooling to room temperature, slides were incubated in a photobleaching buffer for 45 min. After extensive washing in 1× PBS, slides were hydrated and incubated with the conjugated antibody cocktail overnight at 4◦C in a humidity chamber, followed by post-staining fixation and washing steps as instructed in the manufacturer’s user manual. Tissue sections were stored in CODEX Storage Buffer at 4◦C until imaging. Reporter and buffer reagents for PhenoCycler-Fusion imaging were prepared using PhenoCycler Assay Reagent and the 10X Buffer Kit for PhenoCycler-Fusion (Akoya Biosciences) according to the manufacturer’s instructions. High-plex immunofluorescence imaging was then performed on the PhenoCycler-Fusion 2.0 platform. Images were acquired in DAPI, AF488, Atto550, and AF647 channels and automatically processed into multiplexed image files for downstream analysis.

空间蛋白质组分析在 Stanford University 的 Cell Sciences Imaging Facility 使用 PhenoCycler-Fusion（PCF）2.0 平台完成，该平台原名 CODEX。抗体面板包括 Akoya Biosciences 提供的预偶联 CODEX 抗体，以及使用 PhenoCycler Antibody Conjugation Kit 按厂家流程制备的自定义偶联抗体。抗体染色特异性和偶联性能通过免疫荧光和 PhenoCycler-Fusion 测试运行常规验证，并为每种抗体分别优化染色条件。所用抗体列于 Table S1。TMA 和全切片 FFPE 切片使用 PhenoCycler Staining Kit 染色。简要而言，载玻片在 60°C 烘烤过夜后，采用标准组织学流程脱蜡；在压力锅中以 Tris-EDTA 缓冲液（pH 9.0）120°C 高压修复抗原 20 分钟，冷却至室温后在光漂白缓冲液中孵育 45 分钟。使用 1× PBS 充分清洗后，对切片进行水化，并在 4°C 湿盒中与偶联抗体混合液孵育过夜；随后按照厂家手册完成染色后固定与清洗。切片在 4°C 的 CODEX Storage Buffer 中保存至成像。PhenoCycler-Fusion 成像所需报告试剂和缓冲液根据厂家说明使用 PhenoCycler Assay Reagent 与 10X Buffer Kit 制备。随后在 PhenoCycler-Fusion 2.0 平台进行高重数免疫荧光成像，采集 DAPI、AF488、Atto550 和 AF647 通道，并自动处理为供下游分析使用的多重图像文件。

Multiplexed fluorescence images were acquired using the CODEX Instrument Manager at a resolution of 0.507 μm per pixel. Raw image stacks were processed through the CODEX Processor pipeline for cross-cycle image alignment, tile stitching, and autofluorescence subtraction. Processed CODEX images were reviewed in ImageJ (v2.3.0) and QuPath (v0.4.3), and high-quality images were selected for downstream analysis. Each TMA core was treated as a single region of interest (ROI). Processed image stacks subsequently underwent standardized preprocessing and quality control, including tissue detection, ROI curation, and single-cell segmentation on the DAPI channel.84 Nuclear expansion was performed to approximate whole-cell boundaries. Marker expression was quantified as the mean fluorescence intensity within each segmented cell mask. Shape and intensity features were computed for segmented cell compartments, and probability maps were retained to support downstream quality control. In total, over 18 million cells were analyzed across all samples, including over 1.4 million cells from the TMA cohorts and over 16.6 million cells from the WSI cohort.

多重荧光图像由 CODEX Instrument Manager 以 0.507 μm/像素的分辨率采集。原始图像堆栈使用 CODEX Processor 完成跨循环图像对齐、图块拼接和自发荧光扣除。处理后的 CODEX 图像在 ImageJ v2.3.0 和 QuPath v0.4.3 中复核，筛选高质量图像用于后续分析。每个 TMA 核作为一个独立感兴趣区域（ROI）。图像堆栈随后接受标准化预处理和质量控制，包括组织检测、ROI 筛选以及基于 DAPI 通道的单细胞分割。84 通过细胞核外扩近似完整细胞边界；每个分割细胞掩膜内的平均荧光强度作为标志物表达。研究还计算分割细胞区室的形状与强度特征，并保留概率图用于下游质量控制。全部样本共分析超过 1,800 万个细胞，其中 TMA 队列超过 140 万个，WSI 队列超过 1,660 万个。

H&E staining and imaging（H&E 染色与成像）

Following PhenoCycler imaging, flow cells were removed according to the manufacturer’s instructions. Slides were washed extensively with 1× PhenoCycler buffer to remove residual imaging reagents and then processed for post-CODEX H&E staining using a standard histopathology workflow. Briefly, sections were rinsed, stained with hematoxylin, counterstained with eosin, dehydrated, cleared, and coverslipped. H&E-stained sections were digitized using a Leica Aperio AT2 whole-slide scanner at 40× magnification.

完成 PhenoCycler 成像后，按照厂家说明移除流动池。载玻片使用 1× PhenoCycler 缓冲液充分清洗，去除残留成像试剂，再采用标准组织病理流程进行 CODEX 后 H&E 染色。切片依次冲洗、苏木精染色、伊红复染、脱水、透明和封片。H&E 切片使用 Leica Aperio AT2 全切片扫描仪以 40× 放大倍数数字化。

Cell type and cell state annotation using CODEX data（基于 CODEX 数据的细胞类型与状态注释）

Single-cell spatial proteomic data were generated from pixel-level quantification of marker intensities and processed using the Seurat85 package for downstream analysis. To reduce signal variation across markers, data normalization was performed using NormalizeData, followed by feature scaling with ScaleData. Dimensionality reduction was performed by principal component analysis using RunPCA, and sample-level batch effects were corrected with the R package Harmony using RunHarmony. UMAP visualization was generated from the Harmony-corrected embedding, and graph-based clustering was performed using FindNeighbors and FindClusters. Cluster-enriched markers identified using FindAllMarkers were used to assign major lineage identities. Cells with potential segmentation artifacts, ambiguous marker profiles, or clusters lacking clear lineage-defining marker expression were annotated as unknown and were handled separately from annotated cell populations in subsequent analyses. CD45⁺CD3e⁻GZMB⁺ immune cells were annotated as NK-like cells based on the available marker panel and referred to as NK cells in downstream analyses. Detailed functional cell states were annotated by integrating high-resolution clustering with targeted Gaussian mixture modeling86 for selected marker-defined populations, using curated marker combinations derived from the literature. Given the finite marker space of the CODEX panel, a subset of detailed annotations was assigned based on marker-informed phenotypic patterns. For whole-slide CODEX datasets, a two-stage annotation strategy was used. A representative subset of cells from each sample was first annotated to generate a reference set, and anchor-based label transfer was then performed for the remaining cells using FindTransferAnchors and TransferData.

单细胞空间蛋白质组数据来自像素层级标志物强度量化，并使用 Seurat85 进行下游处理。为了降低不同标志物间的信号差异，先使用 NormalizeData 归一化，再用 ScaleData 缩放特征。使用 RunPCA 进行主成分分析降维，并通过 R 包 Harmony 的 RunHarmony 校正样本层级批次效应。在 Harmony 校正后的嵌入上生成 UMAP，并使用 FindNeighbors 和 FindClusters 进行图聚类。通过 FindAllMarkers 识别的簇富集标志物分配主要谱系身份。存在潜在分割伪影、标志物谱不明确或缺乏清晰谱系标志物表达的细胞被注释为 unknown，并与明确注释细胞分开处理。根据现有面板，CD45⁺CD3e⁻GZMB⁺ 免疫细胞被注释为 NK 样细胞，后续统称 NK 细胞。详细功能细胞状态通过高分辨率聚类与针对特定标志物群体的高斯混合模型86相结合进行注释，标志物组合依据文献人工整理。由于 CODEX 面板标志物数量有限，部分精细注释依据标志物信息支持的表型模式分配。对于全切片 CODEX 数据，采用两阶段注释策略：先从每个样本选择代表性细胞并人工/计算注释形成参考集，再使用 FindTransferAnchors 和 TransferData 将标签转移至其余细胞。

Pairwise cell-cell interaction and co-occurrence（细胞间两两相互作用与共现）

To quantify pairwise cell–cell interactions, we applied a radius-based, permutation-derived spatial interaction framework in which all neighboring cells within 40 μm of each cell centroid were defined as the local microenvironment.87 Pairwise interactions between annotated cell phenotypes were assessed independently within each image by comparing the observed frequency of neighboring cell-type pairs with a permutation-based null distribution. To generate the null model, cell phenotypes were randomized 1,000 times while preserving the underlying spatial coordinates. Cell-type pairs enriched relative to the permuted background were interpreted as spatial attraction, whereas depleted pairs were interpreted as spatial avoidance. By evaluating interactions within fixed local neighborhoods, this analysis captures short-range spatial organization and immediate multicellular topology in the TME.

为了量化两两细胞相互作用，研究采用基于半径和置换的空间相互作用框架，将每个细胞质心 40 μm 范围内的全部邻近细胞定义为局部微环境。87 在每张图像中，比较实际观察到的细胞类型邻接频率与置换零分布，独立评估已注释细胞表型之间的两两相互作用。构建零模型时，在保留空间坐标的前提下随机打乱细胞表型 1,000 次。相对于置换背景富集的细胞类型对被解释为空间吸引，减少的细胞对被解释为空间回避。由于分析限定在固定局部邻域内，该方法能够捕捉 TME 中短程空间组织和即时多细胞拓扑。

Cell-cell co-occurrence was quantified at the tissue-core level to assess whether pairs of cell phenotypes were jointly enriched across samples, independent of their physical proximity within individual tissues. This analysis reflects coordinated expansion or depletion of lineages at the tissue scale, revealing global immune or stromal equilibria not detectable from local interactions alone.

细胞共现以组织芯片核为单位进行量化，用于判断不同细胞表型是否在多个样本中共同富集，而不要求它们在单张组织内物理相邻。该分析反映组织尺度上的谱系协同扩增或减少，可揭示局部相互作用无法反映的整体免疫或基质平衡。

Identification of spatial cellular neighborhoods（空间细胞邻域识别）

To characterize multicellular spatial niches within the tumor microenvironment, we applied spatial Latent Dirichlet Allocation (spatialLDA),87,88 a topic modeling framework adapted for spatially resolved cell phenotype data. For each index cell, a local neighborhood was defined as all cells within a 40 μm radius, a scale that consistently captured ∼25 neighboring cells across cohorts and aligns with prior studies delineating spatial domains in tissue microenvironments.89,90

为了刻画 TME 中的多细胞空间生态位，研究采用空间潜在狄利克雷分配（spatial-LDA），87,88这是一种针对空间分辨细胞表型数据改造的主题模型。对每个索引细胞，将 40 μm 半径内全部细胞定义为局部邻域；该尺度在不同队列中稳定覆盖约 25 个邻近细胞，也与既往组织微环境空间区域研究一致。89,90

Spatial-LDA was applied to model local cell composition as mixtures of latent motifs enriched for specific cell types, with spatial neighborhoods defined to retain local tissue context. The resulting motif proportion profiles were subjected to K-means clustering to define CNs, which capture spatially coherent and biologically distinct multicellular ecosystems within the tissue microenvironment. To improve cross-cohort independence and cross-format transferability, CNs were derived by co-clustering each TMA cohort separately with the WSI cohort. To determine the optimal number of CNs, we evaluated candidate solutions (k = 5–20) using complementary validity and stability metrics, including the Silhouette coefficient, Davies–Bouldin index, and Adjusted Rand index calculated between adjacent K partitions.

spatial-LDA 将局部细胞组成建模为多种潜在基序的混合，每种基序富集特定细胞类型，并在定义空间邻域时保留局部组织背景。所得基序比例特征进一步使用 K-means 聚类定义 CN；这些 CN 代表 TME 中空间连贯、具有不同生物学属性的多细胞生态系统。为提高跨队列独立性和跨组织形式可转移性，每个 TMA 队列分别与 WSI 队列联合聚类得到 CN。研究在 k = 5–20 的候选解中，结合轮廓系数、Davies–Bouldin 指数以及相邻 K 值分区间的调整 Rand 指数，评估有效性与稳定性并确定最佳 CN 数量。

Graph-based triplet motif analysis（基于图的三细胞基序分析）

Spatial cell-cell interaction graphs were constructed for each TMA core by connecting CODEX-derived single-cell centroids within a fixed local distance threshold. Each cell was assigned to one of predefined cell types based on single-cell annotations. Local graphs from individual cores were aggregated into a global cell-type interaction network, where nodes represented cell types and edge weights corresponded to pairwise interaction frequencies. To quantify the topological prominence of each cell type, we employed a graph neural network (GNN) approach, implemented using a graph convolutional network (GCN).91 The model used graph topology and interaction weights through a GCN-based architecture to derive node-level topological scores. It was optimized to minimize the mean squared error between predicted and observed node degrees. The resulting node GCN scores captured each cell type’s topological prominence within the global interaction topology.

对每个 TMA 核构建空间细胞相互作用图：将固定局部距离阈值内的 CODEX 单细胞质心连接，并依据单细胞注释为每个细胞分配预定义细胞类型。各组织核的局部图合并为全局细胞类型相互作用网络，其中节点代表细胞类型，边权对应两两相互作用频率。为量化每种细胞类型的拓扑重要性，研究使用图卷积网络（GCN）实现图神经网络（GNN）方法。91 模型利用图拓扑与相互作用权重产生节点层级拓扑评分，并通过最小化预测节点度与实际节点度之间的均方误差训练。最终节点 GCN 评分反映各细胞类型在全局相互作用拓扑中的突出程度。

All possible three-cell-type combinations were exhaustively enumerated, yielding 455 candidate triplet motifs. For each triplet, global network-informed scoring metrics were derived from edge-weight and GCN-based measures of the constituent cell types. Rankings from these metrics were integrated using robust rank aggregation (RRA), and 24 high-confidence triplet motifs with FDR < 0.01 were retained for downstream analysis. At the core level, each retained motif was summarized based on the local spatial organization of its constituent cell types, and motif-level scores were used for subsequent association analyses.

研究穷举全部三细胞类型组合，得到 455 个候选三联体基序。每个三联体的全局网络评分由组成细胞类型的边权和 GCN 指标计算，并使用稳健秩聚合（RRA）整合不同指标排序，保留 FDR < 0.01 的 24 个高置信三联体用于后续分析。在组织核层面，根据组成细胞的局部空间组织汇总每个保留基序，并使用基序评分开展关联分析。

Proximity density and barrier score quantification（邻近密度与屏障评分量化）

Proximity density (PD) quantifies the normalized local interaction density between two specified cell types within a given CN domain, defined as the number of identified proximity interactions divided by the total abundance of those two cell types within the same CN.87 This metric reflects localized cell-type juxtaposition and captures the extent of cell-type co-engagement within spatially constrained tissue niches.

邻近密度（PD）用于量化特定 CN 区域内两种指定细胞类型的归一化局部相互作用密度，定义为识别到的邻近相互作用数量除以同一 CN 中这两类细胞的总丰度。87 该指标反映局部细胞并置程度，刻画空间受限组织生态位中不同细胞类型共同参与的程度。

To quantify stromal-mediated immune exclusion, we adapted the concept of a barrier score from the topological tumor graph framework.92 CD8⁺ T cells, proliferating tumor cells, and CAFs were represented in a spatial graph. For each cytotoxic T cell not directly adjacent to a tumor cell, shortest immune–tumor paths were identified, and each path was classified as barrier-positive if at least one CAF was present along the intervening path. The sample-level barrier score was defined as the fraction of barrier-positive shortest immune–tumor paths across eligible cytotoxic T cells, with higher scores indicating stronger stromal insulation of tumor cells from cytotoxic T cell access.

为了量化基质介导的免疫排斥，研究借鉴拓扑肿瘤图框架构建屏障评分。92 将 CD8⁺ T 细胞、增殖肿瘤细胞和 CAF 表示为空间图。对于每个未直接邻接肿瘤细胞的细胞毒性 T 细胞，寻找最短免疫-肿瘤路径；若中间路径包含至少一个 CAF，则该路径被判定为屏障阳性。样本层级屏障评分定义为所有符合条件的细胞毒性 T 细胞中，屏障阳性最短免疫-肿瘤路径所占比例。评分越高，表示基质对肿瘤细胞与细胞毒性 T 细胞接触的隔离越强。

Habitat label transfer based on CN assignment（基于细胞邻域分配的生境标签转移）

CNs were derived by co-clustering the WSI cohort with TMA1 for discovery and with TMA2 for independent validation. CN labels were transferred to co-registered H&E images as habitat annotations for downstream model training and evaluation. Patch-level training labels were derived from CODEX-based single-cell annotations that were spatially mapped onto the corresponding H&E tissue sections. Patches with evident technical artifacts, including tissue folds, blurred regions, staining artifacts, or image misregistration, were manually excluded during quality control. For each image patch used for model training, the number of cells assigned to each spatial habitat class was quantified. Patches containing five or fewer annotated cells were assigned to a background class to represent noninformative tissue regions. Among habitat-informative patches, only those with more than fifteen annotated cells and a dominant habitat class accounting for at least 60% of the local cellular composition were retained as high-confidence habitat-labeled patches. Each eligible patch was labeled according to its most abundant habitat class. This filtering strategy emphasized the selection of highconfidence and compositionally consistent regions to enhance the reliability of model supervision. A multimodal dataset of spatially co-registered CODEX/H&E image patches (224 × 224 pixels) was assembled for model development. Following quality control, WSI patches were assigned to one of ten CODEX-derived CN labels and split into training (>180K patches) and validation (>82K patches) sets.

Prognostic modeling of habitats on H&E-stained images（H&E 染色图像中生境的预后建模）

To evaluate the prognostic significance of CANVAS-derived spatial habitats, we quantified the habitat profiles of 10 habitats within the tumor bulk and leading-edge compartments of each sample across the TCGA, PLCO, and NLST cohorts. Samples with insufficient patch coverage in both tumor-bulk and leading-edge regions were excluded to ensure adequate spatial representation. Within each histologic subtype and cohort, habitat–outcome associations were evaluated separately using available clinical endpoints, including overall survival (OS), disease-specific survival (DSS), progression-free interval (PFI), and disease-free interval (DFI). For each habitat–compartment combination, univariate Cox proportional hazards models were fitted, and hazard ratios (HRs), 95% confidence intervals, and Wald P values were reported.

为了评估 CANVAS 生境的预后意义，研究在 TCGA、PLCO 和 NLST 队列中，分别量化每个样本肿瘤主体和前缘区的 10 类生境特征。肿瘤主体和前缘区图像块覆盖不足的样本被排除，以保证足够的空间代表性。根据不同组织学亚型和队列可获得的临床终点，分别评估生境与 OS、DSS、PFI 和 DFI 的关联。每个生境-区室组合使用单变量 Cox 比例风险模型，报告 HR、95% CI 和 Wald P 值。

To identify recurrent spatial phenotypes with prognostic relevance, we performed unsupervised consensus clustering of TCGALUAD samples based on CANVAS-derived habitat profiles in tumor bulk and leading-edge regions. A feature matrix comprising the distribution of 10 habitats across the two spatial compartments was z-score normalized and subjected to consensus clustering using the ConsensusClusterPlus R package101 with partitioning around medoids (PAM) and Canberra distance. The resulting consensus-defined subtypes were subsequently linked to clinical variables and survival outcomes. Prognostic value was further assessed using multivariable Cox proportional hazards models adjusted for major clinical covariates, including age, sex, smoking status, and tumor stage. To further contextualize the identified spatial ecotypes, we compared them with transcriptomic TME subtypes assigned to matched samples.38

为识别反复出现且具有预后意义的空间表型，研究根据肿瘤主体和前缘区的 CANVAS 生境特征，对 TCGA-LUAD 样本进行无监督共识聚类。由两个空间区室的 10 类生境分布构成特征矩阵，经 z-score 标准化后，使用 ConsensusClusterPlus101，以围绕中心点划分（PAM）和 Canberra 距离完成共识聚类。所得亚型与临床变量和生存结局关联，并在校正年龄、性别、吸烟状态和肿瘤分期后使用多变量 Cox 模型评估独立预后价值。研究还将空间生态型与匹配样本的转录组 TME 亚型进行比较。38

Orthogonal validation of habitat prediction（生境预测的正交验证）

We assembled orthogonal validation datasets comprising same-section CODEX–H&E specimens and paired Xenium–H&E specimens (Table S2). For Xenium data, preprocessing, clustering, and cell-level annotation were performed separately for each sample using a common Seurat workflow that included quality control, SCTransform normalization, dimensionality reduction, and samplelevel clustering. CN labels were inferred at single-cell resolution using a cosine-similarity-based CN inference framework, in which cell-level neighborhood profiles from spatial omics data were mapped to the most similar CODEX-derived reference CN composition profiles.

研究构建了正交验证数据，包括同切片 CODEX-H&E 标本和配对 Xenium-H&E 标本（Table S2）。Xenium 数据按样本分别使用统一 Seurat 流程处理，包括质量控制、SCTransform 归一化、降维和样本层级聚类。随后使用基于余弦相似度的 CN 推断框架，在单细胞层级推断 CN 标签，将空间组学数据的细胞邻域特征映射至最相似的 CODEX 参考 CN 组成特征。

Gene set enrichment and immunogenomic analysis（基因集富集与免疫基因组分析）

To characterize the tumor microenvironmental correlates of CANVAS-defined spatial habitats, we computed patient-level habitat profiles for each TCGA-NSCLC sample by aggregating patch-level predictions of H01–H10. Samples with limited informative patch coverage were excluded. These spatial features were integrated with three orthogonal TME inference frameworks: (1) ESTIMATE102- derived stromal, immune, and tumor purity scores; (2) CIBERSORTx (LM22)103,104 immune cell scores; and (3) xCell105 enrichment profiles spanning 64 cell-type signatures. All data were harmonized using TCGA sample barcodes to enable multi-omic correlation analysis. The GSVA package106 was used to compute pathway-level enrichment scores from normalized transcriptomic data, leveraging curated gene sets from MSigDB, KEGG, Reactome, and other collections.38 Separately, ecotype-associated pathway enrichment across CANVAS-inferred spatial ecotype groups was assessed using the limma107 R package with empirical Bayes moderation and linear modeling to rank genes for preranked GSEA.

为刻画 CANVAS 空间生境与 TME 的关联，研究汇总每个 TCGA-NSCLC 样本 H01–H10 的图像块预测，计算患者层级生境特征，并排除有效图像块覆盖不足的样本。空间特征与三种独立 TME 推断框架整合：（1）ESTIMATE102 的基质、免疫和肿瘤纯度评分；（2）CIBERSORTx（LM22）103,104 免疫细胞评分；（3）xCell105 的 64 类细胞特征。通过 TCGA 样本条码统一数据后进行多组学相关分析。使用 GSVA106 根据归一化转录组数据和 MSigDB、KEGG、Reactome 等基因集计算通路富集评分。38 另外，使用 limma107 的经验贝叶斯和线性模型评估各空间生态型的差异通路，并为预排序 GSEA 生成基因排序。

Genomic and immunologic correlates, including tumor mutational burden, aneuploidy, microsatellite instability, and SNV-derived neoantigen load, were obtained from public databases. Transcript-based signatures, including interferon response, tertiary lymphoid structure, EMT-TGFβ signaling, and angiogenesis signatures, were compiled from the literature and calculated using ssGSEA as implemented in the GSVA package. Associations between spatial ecotypes and molecular features were assessed using covariateadjusted analysis of variance, with age and sex included as clinical covariates, implemented using the Anova function from the car108 R package.

肿瘤突变负荷、非整倍体、微卫星不稳定性和 SNV 衍生新抗原负荷等基因组与免疫特征来自公开数据库。IFN 反应、TLS、EMT-TGFβ 和血管生成等转录特征根据文献整理，并通过 GSVA 的 ssGSEA 计算。空间生态型与分子特征之间的关联使用包含年龄和性别协变量的方差分析，由 car108 包 Anova 函数实现。

Somatic mutation and copy number alteration association analysis（体细胞突变与拷贝数改变关联分析）

To investigate genomic correlates of CANVAS-defined spatial ecotypes, gene-level copy number alteration and somatic mutation data from the TCGA-LUAD cohort were analyzed. Gene-level copy number calls were obtained from TCGA/cBioPortal processed data and encoded as deletion, neutral, or amplification events. Associations between gene-level copy number status and spatial ecotype membership were assessed using regression-based models. Effect sizes were summarized as odds ratios with corresponding 95% confidence intervals. To facilitate biological interpretation, candidate genes were further annotated according to established oncogenic and immunoregulatory pathways.109

为分析 CANVAS 空间生态型的基因组关联，研究使用 TCGA-LUAD 的基因层级拷贝数改变和体细胞突变数据。拷贝数数据来自 TCGA/cBioPortal 处理结果，并编码为缺失、中性或扩增。通过回归模型分析基因拷贝数状态与空间生态型归属的关系，以 OR 和 95% CI 汇总效应；候选基因再按既有致癌和免疫调节通路注释。109

Somatic mutation profiles were obtained from cBioPortal-derived MC3 consensus calls and binarized at the gene level. For each retained gene, mutation frequencies were calculated within each spatial ecotype, and associations between mutation status and ecotype membership were assessed using Fisher’s exact tests. Recurrent ecotype-associated mutation patterns were visualized by heatmap after z-score normalization of mutation frequencies. Candidate genes were curated from established TCGA PanCancer Atlas driver gene resources.110

体细胞突变来自 cBioPortal 的 MC3 共识数据，并在基因层级二值化。计算各生态型中每个保留基因的突变频率，使用 Fisher 精确检验分析突变状态与生态型之间的关系。突变频率经 z-score 标准化后以热图展示重复出现的生态型相关模式。候选基因依据 TCGA PanCancer Atlas 驱动基因资源整理。110

Habitat-level spatial feature engineering and extraction（生境层级空间特征工程与提取）

To biologically interpret CANVAS-defined spatial habitats, we computed a total of 262 quantitative features from H&E-stained NSCLC tissue sections. All features were derived from patch-level annotations and subsequently aggregated to the image level to enable cohort-wide comparative analyses. (1) Composition features (n = 10): patch-level CANVAS annotations were aggregated per image to quantify the frequency of each of the ten spatial habitats. (2) Diversity features (n = 6): to capture ecological complexity within each image, we computed six diversity metrics using habitat label counts as species abundances, including richness, Shannon index, Simpson index, inverse Simpson index, Fisher’s alpha, and Pielou’s evenness. (3) Spatial dispersion features (n = 90): to characterize intra-habitat spatial organization, we converted habitat-specific coordinates into planar point patterns and derived a set of spatial summary features for each habitat, including summaries from Ripley’s K and L functions, pair correlation, G, F, and J functions, together with the Clark–Evans index, quadrat-based dispersion statistics, and kernel density summaries. (4) Interaction features (n = 100): to evaluate inter-habitat spatial coupling, pairwise interaction scores were computed for each tissue image using a fixed-radius neighborhood framework. For each habitat pair, interaction scores were derived using a permutation-based scoring approach (1,000 iterations), comparing observed spatial adjacency frequencies with a randomized null distribution. This yielded 100 ordered habitat-pair interaction features corresponding to the full 10 × 10 interaction matrix. (5) Distance features (n = 55): pairwise nearest-neighbor Euclidean distances between habitat pairs were calculated for each tissue image and summarized as imagelevel spatial distance features. (6) Transition feature (n = 1): Spatial transition entropy (STE) was computed to quantify the habitat transition complexity within each image. For each tissue image, habitat transitions were defined based on patch-level k-nearestneighbor relationships (k = 6), and a habitat transition matrix was constructed from all observed transitions between neighboring patches. STE was then calculated as the Shannon entropy of the resulting global transition probability distribution.

为解释 CANVAS 空间生境的生物学意义，研究从 NSCLC H&E 组织切片中计算 262 个定量特征。全部特征来自图像块注释，并汇总至图像层级用于队列比较。（1）组成特征（n = 10）：汇总每张图像中 10 类生境的频率。（2）多样性特征（n = 6）：以生境标签计数作为物种丰度，计算丰富度、Shannon 指数、Simpson 指数、逆 Simpson 指数、Fisher α 和 Pielou 均匀度。（3）空间离散特征（n = 90）：将各生境坐标转换为平面点模式，提取 Ripley K、L、配对相关、G、F、J 函数、Clark–Evans 指数、样方离散统计和核密度等摘要。（4）相互作用特征（n = 100）：使用固定半径邻域框架和 1,000 次置换比较实际邻接与随机零分布，计算每个生境对的相互作用评分，得到完整 10 × 10 有向相互作用矩阵。（5）距离特征（n = 55）：计算各生境对最近邻欧氏距离并汇总到图像层级。（6）转移特征（n = 1）：空间转移熵（STE）用于量化图像内生境转移复杂性；根据图像块 k 近邻关系（k = 6）定义生境转移，构建转移矩阵，再计算全局转移概率分布的 Shannon 熵。

Feature selection and model development（特征选择与模型开发）

To construct a robust model for predicting immunotherapy outcomes, we implemented a multi-stage computational pipeline encompassing feature curation, redundancy reduction, feature prioritization, and survival modeling. As an unsupervised preprocessing step, multicollinearity was first reduced by calculating the Spearman correlation matrix across all predictors. Features with absolute pairwise correlation > 0.95 were grouped by Louvain community detection using the igraph package,111 and one representative feature from each cluster was retained to generate a reduced, non-collinear feature set.

为构建稳健的免疫治疗结局预测模型，研究采用多阶段流程，包括特征整理、冗余削减、特征优先级排序和生存建模。首先进行无监督预处理：计算全部预测变量的 Spearman 相关矩阵，将绝对两两相关系数 > 0.95 的特征使用 igraph111 的 Louvain 社区检测分组，每组保留一个代表特征，形成低共线性特征集。

Robust candidate predictors were then prioritized using two complementary resampling-based approaches. First, we performed 100 repeated resampling iterations of LASSO-penalized Cox regression using the glmnet package.112 In each iteration, patients were randomly divided into training and validation subsets, and the training subset was used for model fitting with internal 5-fold cross-validation to determine the optimal penalty parameter based on the concordance index. Features ranked in the top 10% by selection frequency were retained. Second, we applied permutation-based random survival forest analysis using the randomForestSRC package. Across 1,000 bootstrap iterations, feature importance was quantified by permuting individual predictors and measuring the resulting change in prediction error for progression-free survival. Features showing consistently high permutation importance were prioritized as robust predictors.

随后使用两种互补的重采样方法筛选稳健候选预测因素。第一，使用 glmnet112 进行 100 次重复重采样的 LASSO-Cox 回归。每次随机将患者划分为训练和验证子集，训练集内部使用 5 折交叉验证，根据一致性指数确定最佳惩罚参数；按入选频率排名前 10% 的特征被保留。第二，使用 randomForestSRC 进行基于置换的随机生存森林分析。通过 1,000 次 bootstrap，逐一置换预测变量并衡量 PFS 预测误差变化，计算特征重要性；持续具有较高置换重要性的特征被优先保留。

Thirteen features jointly prioritized by the LASSO and random survival forest procedures were subsequently used to train a multivariable Cox proportional hazards model. Model coefficients were estimated using the available cohort and model performance was evaluated using the C-index and time-dependent AUCs at 6, 12, and 24 months. For survival visualization, patients were stratified into risk groups based on the median risk score within the corresponding cohort. Prognostic separation was summarized using hazard ratios, log-rank P values, and 95% confidence intervals. Final model coefficients were visualized in a forest plot with annotation categories.

LASSO 与随机生存森林共同优先选出的 13 个特征用于训练多变量 Cox 比例风险模型。模型系数使用现有队列估计，以 C-index 以及 6、12、24 个月时间依赖 AUC 评估性能。生存可视化时，根据相应队列风险评分中位数将患者分为高低风险组，并报告 HR、log-rank P 值和 95% CI。最终模型系数在带类别注释的森林图中展示。

QUANTIFICATION AND STATISTICAL ANALYSIS（定量与统计分析）

All statistical analyses were conducted using R (version 4.4.0) and Python (version 3.10) for data preprocessing, visualization, and hypothesis testing. Two-group comparisons were performed using two-sided t tests or Mann–Whitney U tests, as appropriate. For comparisons involving more than two groups, ANOVA or non-parametric Kruskal–Wallis tests were applied as appropriate. P values were adjusted for multiple comparisons using the Benjamini–Hochberg false discovery rate (FDR) procedure where applicable. Survival analysis was performed using Kaplan–Meier estimators with log-rank tests, and survival associations were further evaluated using univariate and multivariable Cox proportional hazards models implemented in the survival R package. Unless otherwise specified, all tests were two-sided, and P < 0.05 was considered statistically significant.

全部统计分析使用 R 4.4.0 和 Python 3.10 完成，包括数据预处理、可视化和假设检验。两组比较根据适用条件使用双侧 t 检验或 Mann–Whitney U 检验；多组比较使用 ANOVA 或非参数 Kruskal–Wallis 检验。需要多重比较时，使用 Benjamini–Hochberg FDR 方法校正 P 值。生存分析采用 Kaplan–Meier 估计和 log-rank 检验，并使用 survival R 包中的单变量和多变量 Cox 比例风险模型进一步评估生存关联。除特别说明外，全部检验均为双侧，P < 0.05 视为具有统计学意义。
